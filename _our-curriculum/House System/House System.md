<p>The House System has been a proud tradition at Blangah Rise Primary School
since its founding. Re-established in 2021, it continues to strengthen
school spirit and foster empowering teacher-student relationships that
support every child's growth.</p>
<p>Our four Houses — Delta, Gillman, Keppel, and Redhill — are named in honour
of Singapore's heritage. Each House carries a piece of the nation's history
and embodies virtues that guide our students to grow as both independent
and inter-dependent learners, developing a strong sense of identity, belonging,
and civic consciousness along the way.</p>
<p>From 2021 to 2025, Primary 3 to Primary 6 students participated in Modular
Sports (House Practice), developing skills across Athletics, Dance, Floorball,
and Volleyball. In 2026, the House System was enhanced to welcome Primary
1 and Primary 2 students into the fold. Modular Sports have since evolved
into House Days, where students from Primary 1 to Primary 6 come together
in level activities grouped by their respective Houses. Through these shared
experiences, students develop teamwork, communication, and resilience —
learning to collaborate across differences, support one another, and strive
towards common goals.</p>
<p>As a platform that supports our total school curriculum, the House System
remains firmly anchored in the RISE Values. It is designed to nurture students
who are confident, adaptable, and socially aware — individuals equipped
with the values, skills, and competencies to thrive in an ever-changing
world, and to contribute meaningfully to the communities they are part
of.</p>
