<p>Our Primary 2 students embarked on an exciting learning journey to the
Flower Dome as part of <em>Wise Wee and the Little Adventurers</em>!</p>
<p>Throughout the experience, students were encouraged to observe, explore
and discover the natural world around them. Armed with tablets, they participated
in interactive activities as they explored the Flower Dome, making their
learning both engaging and meaningful.</p>
<p></p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 60%;" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/2026 Photo Album/P2 LJ/Photo_3.jpg">
</div>
<p>Along the way, students encountered fascinating plants from different
parts of the world and discovered their unique features and importance
to other living things. Through hands-on exploration and purposeful use
of technology, they developed a deeper appreciation for the wonders of
nature.</p>
<p>It was a memorable morning filled with learning, exploration and discovery
beyond the classroom!</p>
