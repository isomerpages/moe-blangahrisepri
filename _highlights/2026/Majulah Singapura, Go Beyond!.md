<p>On 7 August, our school came together to celebrate Singapore’s 61st birthday
with this year’s theme, “Majulah Singapura, Go Beyond!”</p>
<p>The celebrations began with a March-In, followed by the singing of our
National Anthem and the recitation of the National Pledge. As we commemorated
the 60th anniversary of the Singapore Pledge, students reflected on its
meaning through engaging classroom discussions and explored how they could
put the values of the Pledge into action through their everyday choices
and actions.</p>
<p>The celebration continued in the school hall with the National Day message,
moments of recollection, energising songs, mass exercise and an exciting
quiz, bringing our school community together in the spirit of joy and togetherness.</p>
<p>Through these meaningful experiences, our students were reminded that
each of us has a part to play in building a stronger and more united Singapore.</p>
<p>Happy 61st Birthday, Singapore! Together, let us go beyond, move forward
and continue building One People, One Nation, One Singapore.</p>
