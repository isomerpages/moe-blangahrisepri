<p>Blangah Rise has many platforms for our pupils to be an active learner
through our CCAs, Sports activities and House Sessions. In these sessions,
students experience joyful&nbsp;learning with their friends, and they collaborate
actively to achieve team goals. These multiple learning platforms help
your child to shine. We believe that having a healthy body is the foundation
for a child to grow. SATS, Asia’s leading airline caterer, has been engaged
to provide healthy and nutritious school meals for the students. A dietician
on the SATS team ensures that all meals adhere to HPB’s Healthy Meals in
School Programme and offer the optimal amount of nutrients.</p>

<p>The taller and bigger a tree becomes, the deeper its roots take hold.
Your roots are the foundation for your growth in life. In Blangah Rise
Primary School, we believe that Virtues and the 7 Habits serve as a foundation
for our children’s growth in life. We help every student see the “Leader
in Me'' and also the power of peer support through the “Leader in Us”.
These efforts are woven intentionally into every aspect of school life
in BRPS, from the classroom to CCAs and after school programmes.</p>
