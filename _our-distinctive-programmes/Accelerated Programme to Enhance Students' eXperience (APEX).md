<p><strong>A</strong>ccelerated <strong>P</strong>rogramme to <strong>E</strong>nhance
students’ e<strong>X</strong>perience (<strong>APEX</strong>) is designed
to stretch Primary 3 to Primary 6 students with academic strengths and
talents, while nurturing confident leaders and self-directed learners.
Through enriched learning experiences, APEX aims to ignite curiosity, deepen
thinking and develop essential 21st Century Competencies that prepare students
to thrive in a rapidly changing world.</p>
<h4><strong>Student Identification and Placement</strong></h4>
<p>To ensure students with high potential are appropriately stretched, APEX
uses a holistic identification process. This goes beyond academic scores
to evaluate learning dispositions and cognitive readiness, ensuring that
students with specific strengths and talents in English, Math, and Science
are matched with the right level of enrichment. Student placement is reviewed
annually to ensure the programme continues to align with each student’s
developmental needs and learning progress.</p>
<h4><strong>English&nbsp;Language</strong></h4>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/2026 Photo Album/APEX 2026/Debate_4.jpg">
</div>
<p>APEX (English Language) comprises three distinctive threads: Scrabble,
Writing and Debate, which are offered across P3-P6 levels. With their memorised
Latin, Greek roots and two-letter words, our Scrabblers who love wordplay
are trained to compete against students from other schools. Through rigorous
trainings, our Scrabblers hone their craft in critical, adaptive and strategic
thinking. They have brought home many Individual and Team Championship
medals over the years.</p>
<p></p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/2026 Photo Album/APEX 2026/Scabble__new_.jpg">
</div>
<p>Those with a flair for writing are given opportunities to develop their
creative and journalistic skills. As part of their in-curriculum stretch,
our High Ability English Learners come together in the classroom to pen <strong><em>BRavo!</em></strong>&nbsp;–
our BRPS student e-newsletter, for the students, by the students. Guided
their EL teachers, our APEX students share their creative writing, book
reviews, and thoughts about contemporary issues they care about. Read our
latest issue of&nbsp;<strong><em>BRavo!</em></strong>&nbsp;<a href="https://www.blangahrisepri.moe.edu.sg/announcements/publications/bravo/" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">here.</a>
</p>
<p></p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">

<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">

<p>Finally, debating is a strategy we employ for our High Ability English
Learners to stretch their oracy and critical thinking skills. At the upper
primary levels, students are given the opportunity to pit their wits against
each other in argumentative debate on topics such as eSports, kid influencers,
as well as the banes and boons of video games. In 2025, our school debaters
emerged one of the Top 3 teams at the Wits &amp; Words Inter-School Debate
Competition (Division II).</p>

<p>In APEX (STEM), students engage in inquiry-based STEM learning experiences
that develop reasoning, problem-solving and innovative thinking. Through
hands-on exploration, students deepen their understanding of scientific
concepts by:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Designing creative toys and innovations</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Experimenting with flight and aerodynamics using drones</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Exploring emerging technologies, including AI-related applications, under
expert guidance</p>
</li>
</ul>

<p>Students are encouraged to ask questions, brainstorm ideas, build prototypes
and test solutions, fostering resilience, adaptability and a growth mindset.
These authentic learning experiences strengthen 21<sup>st</sup> Century
Competencies such as critical, adaptive and inventive thinking as well
as communication, collaboration and information skills.</p>
