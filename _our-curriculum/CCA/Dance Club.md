<p>Our Dance CCA equips students with fundamental skills in balance, coordination,
flexibility, and musicality. Beyond technical development, it nurtures
character excellence by instilling confidence, discipline, and teamwork.</p>
<p></p>
<p>Students have ample opportunities to showcase their talents, from the
Singapore Youth Festival (SYF) Arts Presentation to performances at internal
and external events such as the School Open House and BRLC Celebrates!.
Notably, our Dance Club members have had the honour of performing at the
Telok Blangah National Carnival for two consecutive years.</p>
<p></p>
<p>Dance CCA also offers a meaningful pathway for students to pursue their
passion and develop a lifelong appreciation for the arts. Valerie Huang
from 5 Sapphire shares that she enjoys Dance CCA for the opportunity to
learn new techniques and experience the joy of performing on stage alongside
her friends. Jereca Choy from 4 Sapphire, who joined the school last year,
chose Dance as her CCA and looks forward to every session with enthusiasm.
She particularly enjoys dancing with her teammates and is excited to be
taking part in the SYF Dance Presentation for the very first time in 2026.</p>
