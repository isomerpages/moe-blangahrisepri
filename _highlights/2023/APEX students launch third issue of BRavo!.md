<p>As part of the English Department's aim to give students a voice to share
their thoughts and views through writing, we welcome the third edition
of BRavo!, a publication by students for students. Over several months
of meetings, our budding writers in the APEX-EL Writing Programme had fun
stretching&nbsp;their imagination&nbsp;and writing potential to put this
newsletter together. This issue comprises a collection of our students'
creative and journalistic works. We hope you enjoy reading it.</p>
