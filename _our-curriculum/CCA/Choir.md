<p>The Blangah Rise Primary School Choir, Rising Harmonies, provides students
with an enriching musical journey through choral singing. Our members develop
strong vocal techniques whilst learning the importance of teamwork and
musical excellence.</p>
<p>Through a diverse repertoire and regular training sessions, choir members
cultivate listening skills, vocal control, and ensemble harmony. Beyond
musicianship, the programme nurtures essential 21st Century Competencies
— students develop communication, collaboration, and self-management as
they learn to blend their voices, respond to one another, and perform with
precision and expression.</p>
<p>Rising Harmonies actively participates in school and community celebrations,
providing valuable performance experience. The students showcase their
talents at external events including the Voices of Singapore choral festival
and Singapore Youth Festival, connecting with the broader choral community.&nbsp;</p>
<p>Through music-making, our students grow in confidence, resilience, and
a sense of shared purpose. The friendships forged and the pride of performing
together make Rising Harmonies not just a musical experience, but a deeply
personal and memorable one.</p>
