<p><strong>Creating a Caring and Enabling School Environment for All At BRPS</strong>
</p>
<p>We create opportunities for every student to exercise student agency and
positively influence others through compassionate action and responsible
leadership.</p>
<p></p>
<p><strong>Our Approach to Student Discipline</strong>
</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 70%;" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/2024 Photos/SM /SM_2.png">
</div>
<p>The school’s character development programme – Leader in Me – centres
on Stephen Covey's Seven Habits of Happy Kids and The Virtues Project.
Through this programme, we instil self-discipline in our students by guiding
them to embody virtues for personal growth and interpersonal effectiveness.</p>
<p>Furthermore, we support the development of students’ social and emotional
skills as well as those with learning and behavioural needs through multi-pronged
values-based approaches. These approaches along with a positive, educative
and restorative approach to discipline ensure that our students stay healthy
and positive and are competent and confident to achieve success in school.\</p>
<p><strong>BRPS START RIGHT PROGRAMME</strong>
</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 50%;" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/2026 Photo Album/Student Management/Photo_2.jpg">
</div>
<p>The BRPS Start RIGHT Programme sets the tone for the new academic year.
Through this key annual initiative, form teachers engage students in purposeful
activities centred on student wellness, classroom management, and the building
of positive relationships — all in service of a conducive learning environment
for every child.</p>
<p>Students take part in a curated range of activities designed to build
rapport with their peers and teachers, and to establish shared goals and
expectations.</p>
<p>Among these is the Good Words Campaign, which champions the use of kind
and affirming language in everyday interactions.</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 70%;" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/2024 Photos/SM /SM_3.png">
</div>
<p>To be more purposeful in their planning of activities beyond school hours,
students are also mentored by their teachers to plan a schedule that not
only consists of homework and revisions, but one that also champions good
practice of self-care.</p>
<p><strong>The RISE Student Leadership Programme</strong>
</p>
<p></p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/2026 Photo Album/Student Management/Image_3.png">
</div>
<p>At Blangah Rise Primary School, The RISE Student Leadership Programme
develops every child to lead with Respect, Integrity, Self-Discipline and
Empathy. Through a whole-school, tiered approach, students grow in self-leadership,
take on class and school leadership roles, and, for some, progress to the
highest levels of student leadership.</p>

<p>The programme is anchored by the RISE Student Leadership Pyramid and the
RISE Student Leadership Framework. Students are progressively developed
through class roles, school appointments, coaching, reflection and authentic
opportunities to lead, serve and contribute to a caring and influence others
through compassionate action and responsible leadership.</p>
<p></p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/2026 Photo Album/Student Management/Image_6.png">
</div>
<p></p>
<p>Leadership development is further strengthened through our Learning for
Life Programme (LLP), where students develop eco-stewardship, student voice
and the emerging 21CC. Through meaningful projects, school platforms and
guided experiences, students learn to communicate confidently, work with
others, take initiative and contribute purposefully to the school and community.</p>

<p><strong>Growing Eco-Stewardship</strong>
</p>
<p>Our student leaders take their environmental advocacy beyond the classroom,
leading storytelling sessions at pre-schools and other community spaces
to champion causes close to the earth. These sessions give them authentic
platforms to hone their communication skills and civic literacy, inspiring
young audiences to care for the environment and appreciate biodiversity.</p>
<p>The Eco-Stewards also lead participants in hands-on art and craft sessions,
where creations are made entirely from recycled materials – turning sustainability
into a lived experience. From crafting props to preparing session materials,
their deep involvement in every stage of the process has further ignited
their passion for environmental stewardship and their commitment to caring
for Mother Earth.</p>
