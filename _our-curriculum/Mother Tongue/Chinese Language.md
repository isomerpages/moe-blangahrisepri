<p><strong>Teaching and Learning</strong>
</p>
<p>Learning of the Chinese Language is supported through a wide range of
thoughtfully designed lesson activities that cater to students’ different
learning needs and strengths. Students engage with learning materials in
multiple ways, whether through hands-on tasks, written exercises, discussions,
or digital activities using tools such as the Student Learning Space (SLS).
These varied approaches allow students to explore concepts at their own
pace, practise new skills, and express their understanding in ways that
suit them best.</p>
<p>By combining different modes of learning, lessons become more interactive
and meaningful, helping students stay motivated and engaged. Students are
encouraged to take an active role in their learning, reflect on their progress,
and apply what they have learned in different contexts. This flexible approach
ensures that all students can participate fully and develop confidence
and competence in the Chinese Language.</p>

<p><strong>Chinese Language Fortnight and Chinese Language Camp</strong>
</p>
<p>Every year, the Chinese Language Fortnight and P4 Chinese Camp are organised
to provide students with meaningful and engaging platforms for experiential
learning. Through a variety of cultural and language-based activities such
as traditional handicraft making, Chinese calligraphy, face mask painting,
language games (e.g. idioms and riddles) and performing arts such as allegro,
students experience and appreciate the Chinese language and culture in
an authentic and enjoyable way.</p>
<p>The camp, designed for P4 students, deepens their understanding of Chinese
culture and traditions while encouraging the frequent and meaningful use
of the Chinese Language. An engaging learning environment is created to
support students in using the language confidently in real-life contexts.</p>

<p><strong>MTL Challenge (Chinese Language)</strong>
</p>
<p>Learning takes place when students can apply what they have learnt in
their classrooms. As such, MTL Challenges (Chinese Language) are organised
for our students every semester. These internal competitions vary from
speaking to writing, and students get to pit their creativity and knowledge
against each other.</p>
<p>Outstanding performances are recognised through specially designed medals,
to celebrate students’ efforts and achievements.</p>

<p><strong>Chinese Reading Programme</strong>
</p>
<p>The Chinese Reading Programme aims to cultivate students’ reading habits,
spark their interest in Chinese reading, and develop their language and
21st-century competencies.</p>
<p>Students across levels take part in themed reading activities linked to
everyday life, values and curriculum texts, including Chinese classics
and historical stories. Through teacher-guided reading, discussions and
simple creative tasks, students learn to listen, speak, read and write
in an integrated way.</p>
<p>Students share their learning through reading journals, short book creations
and storytelling activities. Parents also participate in the programme
by supporting storytelling sessions, helping to build a positive and supportive
reading culture in school.</p>
