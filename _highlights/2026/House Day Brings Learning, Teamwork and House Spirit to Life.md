<p>The new term began with excitement as students across all levels came
together for House Day, taking part in a variety of meaningful and engaging
activities that fostered teamwork, resilience, creativity, and school spirit.</p>
<p>Our Primary 1 students embraced "The Great Straw Tower Challenge", working
together as junior engineers to design and build the tallest straw towers.
Through perseverance, problem-solving, and collaboration, they discovered
that success is built on teamwork and determination.</p>
<p>Primary 2 students put the physical skills they had developed during PE
lessons into action through a series of energetic relay challenges. Filled
with enthusiastic cheers and friendly competition, the event celebrated
active living, resilience, and outstanding sportsmanship.</p>
<p>Primary 3 students enjoyed a refreshing Walk-A-Jog at Telok Blangah Hill
Park, promoting healthy living while strengthening friendships in the great
outdoors.</p>
<p>For Primary 4, learning extended beyond the classroom as students created
upcycled PET bottle terrariums. Through this hands-on activity, they explored
concepts such as photosynthesis, respiration, and soil composition, while
developing a deeper appreciation for environmental sustainability through
recycling and upcycling.</p>
<p>Our Senior Primary students rounded off the celebrations with exciting
House-based activities. Primary 5 students participated in a variety of
interactive game stations, while Primary 6 students showcased their teamwork
and determination in modified softball matches. Beyond the friendly competition,
the activities strengthened camaraderie, communication, and the sense of
belonging within each House.</p>
<p>House Day was a wonderful reminder that learning happens in many ways
- through play, collaboration, exploration, and shared experiences. It
was an inspiring start to the new term, bringing our school community together
while nurturing confident, caring, and resilient learners.</p>
