<p>Welcome to the Senior Primary webpage!</p>
<p>We warmly welcome you and your child and look forward to working closely
with you as valued partners in your child’s learning journey. Through open
communication and strong collaboration, we strive to nurture every student’s
holistic development, academically, socially, and emotionally, so that
they may grow with confidence and joy.</p>
<p>To support your child’s learning, we encourage families to ensure adequate
rest, foster responsibility in preparing school materials, and cultivate
good study habits through the timely completion of homework and regular
revision. These practices help students remain focused, well-prepared,
and confident in their learning.</p>
<p>By working together, we can make a meaningful and positive impact on your
child’s holistic development and support them in achieving their fullest
potential. We look forward to partnering with you to foster a supportive
and enriching learning environment.</p>
<p>Please stay updated on this webpage for information on upcoming activities
and events at the Senior Primary level. Thank you for your continued support
and commitment to your child’s education.</p>

<p><strong>TSR Time in Senior Primary</strong>
</p>
<p>Teachers and students begin a typical school day by engaging in fun-filled
activities and meaningful conversations. During Teacher–Student Relationship
(TSR) time, teachers not only take the opportunity to bond with their students
but also create spaces for students to build camaraderie among themselves.</p>
<p>Teachers highlight positive behaviours displayed by students and affirm
their actions through a blend of engaging activities and purposeful conversations.
This dedicated time helps to foster trust, strengthen relationships, and
set a positive tone for the day ahead.</p>
<p>“TSR time is a great way to build meaningful relationships with my students.
I am able to strengthen my bond with my class before lessons begin and
interact freely with them. Most importantly, it starts the day on a cheerful
and engaging note that keeps them motivated throughout the day,” shared
Ms Munirah.</p>

<p><strong>A glimpse into our Primary 5 and 6 classrooms</strong>
</p>
<p>A large portion of a student’s time is spent in the classroom. It is therefore
important to create a learning space that is vibrant and inviting, so that
learning can take place in a comfortable and safe environment.</p>
<p>Our students and teachers take pride in and ownership of the classrooms
they use. They work together to decorate the space and put up posters and
notices that support learning and reflect class identity. These collaborative
efforts help to foster a sense of belonging and responsibility among students.</p>

<p><strong>P6 Learning Journey to Sungei Buloh</strong>
</p>
<p>What better way to learn about the rich diversity of living things than
by bringing students into nature for authentic, first-hand experiences?
Beyond classroom videos, real-world learning allows concepts to truly come
alive.</p>
<p>Our Primary 6 students visited Sungei Buloh Wetland Reserve to explore
the unique characteristics of mangrove ecosystems. Through this learning
journey, they deepened their understanding of food chains and food webs,
animal adaptations, and the impact of human activities on the environment.</p>

<p><strong>P6 Post PSLE Programme</strong>
</p>
<p>The Post-PSLE period marks a meaningful transition for our students, a
time to pause, explore, and grow beyond the examination journey. Through
our carefully designed Post-PSLE Programmes (PPP), we aim to nurture well-rounded
individuals by providing enriching experiences that develop both heart
and mind.</p>
<p>Our programmes are thoughtfully designed by our teachers, ensuring holistic
development for every learner. Through hands-on activities, meaningful
reflections, and shared experiences, PPP provides our students with the
opportunity to discover new interests, build lasting skills, and step confidently
into the next chapter of their educational journey.</p>
