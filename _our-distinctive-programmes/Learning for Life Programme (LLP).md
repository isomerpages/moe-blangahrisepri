<p><strong>Character First, Leadership for Life</strong>
</p>
<p>At Blangah Rise Primary School, our Learning for Life Programme — Leaders
for Life — is a six-year journey that develops students into caring, confident
and responsible leaders. Grounded in environmental education and student
leadership, every child grows in character, learns to collaborate, and
takes meaningful action beyond the classroom.</p>
<p>Anchored in our school values RISE — Respect, Integrity, Self-Discipline
and Empathy — the programme nurtures environmental stewardship as a daily
practice.</p>

<p><strong>Our Approach: The 3Es — Educate. Enrich. Empower.</strong>
</p>
<p><strong>(1) Educate — Build Knowledge and Awareness</strong>
</p>
<p>Students learn sustainability concepts connected to real-life issues such
as waste reduction, water conservation and eco-responsibility. Student
leaders reinforce these messages through school-wide campaigns as shown
below to encourage water-saving habits at home.</p>

<p><strong>(2) Enrich — Hands-On, Meaningful Experiences</strong>
</p>
<p>Students learn that sustainability is not abstract — it is practised through
everyday habits and routines. Through our school's recycling ecosystem,
students learn to sort recyclables correctly and understand why responsible
recycling matters.</p>
<p></p>

<p>Beyond the classroom, students participate in hands-on workshops, nature-based
learning journeys and sustainability programmes aligned to Singapore's
Green Plan 2030. In one programme, students design, build and race solar-powered
cars, discovering how renewable energy works in real life.</p>

<p><strong>(3) Empower — Student Agency and Leadership</strong>
</p>
<p>Students are empowered to lead and create impact through authentic roles.
Green Ambassadors, part of the class committee, model our RISE values by
championing sustainable habits, guiding peers and supporting school-wide
efforts to reduce waste, conserve water and improve energy efficiency.</p>

<p>Our LLP also extends into the home and community. Through Project D.R.O.P.
and community outreach platforms, students bring sustainability messages
beyond the school gates. Collaborations with external organisations give
students the opportunity to design and lead their own environmental awareness
initiatives.</p>

<p><strong>What Students Gain</strong>
</p>
<p>Through Leaders for Life, every student develops the character, skills
and confidence to make a difference — for the environment, for others,
and for life.</p>
