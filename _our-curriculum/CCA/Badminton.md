<p>BRPS Badminton CCA welcomes students of all skill levels, from beginners
to competitive players. Through CCA, students develop technical skills
as well as character traits such as determination and perseverance. Our
school provides students with opportunities to represent the school in
National School Games (NSG) in both the Senior and Junior Divisions.</p>
<p></p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/2026 Photo Album/Badminton/B2.jpg">
</div>
<p>NSG provides students with valuable learning experiences that grow them
in spirit, body and mind. A number of student-athletes go on to pursue
Badminton in their choice secondary schools through the Direct School Admission
(DSA) exercise.</p>
<p>Beyond sports achievements, the BRPS Badminton Program emphasizes character
building. It fosters resilience, teamwork, and sportsmanship through intentional
coaching and mentoring.</p>
