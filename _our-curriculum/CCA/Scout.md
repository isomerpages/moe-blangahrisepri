<p>BRPS Cub Scouts is a programme rooted in character, service, and leadership.
At every CCA session, members begin by reciting the Scout Promise and Scout
Law, grounding themselves in the values of discipline, responsibility,
and integrity that define the scouting tradition.</p>
<p>Through hands-on activities such as knot-tying, tent-pitching, track and
trail, and basic first aid, Cub Scouts develop essential scouting skills
through experiential learning. Working within their packs, they build critical
thinking, communication, and collaboration as they plan, problem-solve,
and strive towards shared goals — competencies that prepare them well beyond
the CCA room.</p>
<p>Leadership is woven into the fabric of the programme. Through regular
leadership roles, Scout Leadership Camps, and participation in school and
district activities, Cub Scouts are given meaningful opportunities to develop
confidence, initiative, and a sense of responsibility towards others. They
also serve the wider school and community through events such as the National
Day Marching Ceremony, Commemoration of Scout Founder's Day, and the Singapore
Scouts Community Challenge fund-raising efforts.</p>
<p>Through these rich and purposeful experiences, BRPS Cub Scouts grow into
resilient, adaptable, and service-oriented leaders — individuals of strong
character who are ready to contribute meaningfully to their community and
the world beyond.</p>
