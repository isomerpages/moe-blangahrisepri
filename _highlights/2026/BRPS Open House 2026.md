<p>BRPS welcomed prospective families to its annual Open House, offering
parents and children an opportunity to experience the school's vibrant
learning environment and discover the many opportunities available to every
child.</p>
<p>Throughout the morning, visitors explored interactive classroom activities,
met our dedicated teachers and learnt more about the school's academic
programmes, co-curricular activities and student development initiatives.
Preschoolers enjoyed a variety of hands-on activities specially designed
to spark their curiosity, while parents had the opportunity to engage teachers
in meaningful conversations and gain a deeper understanding of learning
at BRPS.</p>
<p>The Principal &amp; Teachers' Dialogue provided parents with valuable
insights into the school's vision, values and distinctive programmes. Parents
actively participated in the dialogue, raising thoughtful questions about
the school's curriculum, learning environment and student development.
The Student Dialogue complemented these conversations by giving visitors
the opportunity to hear directly from our students about their experiences,
friendships and life at BRPS.</p>
<p>Visitors were also treated to captivating performances by our Choir and
Dance groups, while the Debate Showcase highlighted our students' confidence,
critical thinking and communication skills.</p>
<p>Adding to the warm and welcoming atmosphere, our student ambassadors proudly
led guided tours around the school and shared their personal experiences,
giving families an authentic glimpse into everyday life at BRPS.</p>
<p>We hope our Open House gave families a meaningful insight into the caring
and vibrant BRPS community. We look forward to welcoming our new Primary
1 students and partnering with their families as they embark on this exciting
educational journey.</p>
