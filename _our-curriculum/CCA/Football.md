<p>At BRPS, football is purposeful. Student-athletes are grouped by ability
so that training can be customised and differentiated for optimal growth.
Every CCA session is thoughtfully planned, progressing from warm-up through
technical drills, tactical play, and match simulations. Our emphasis on
safety and discipline ensures that student-athletes grow holistically in
values, knowledge, and skills.</p>
<p></p>
<p>Selection to the school team is based on a holistic assessment of both
aptitude and attitude.. Guided by dedicated teachers and coaches, our student-athletes
continue to develop not just in skill, but in confidence, humility, and
sportsmanship — qualities that will serve them both on and off the pitch.
Our teachers draw on the stories and journeys of football legends and role
models to inspire our boys, instilling in them the belief that with hard
work, resilience, and determination, anything is possible.</p>
