<p><strong>Vision:</strong> A creative and vibrant Arts environment which
cultivates every child to be competent and confident, with an appreciation
for the Arts.</p>
<p><strong>Mission:</strong>
</p>
<p>1. To encourage appreciation of the arts</p>
<p>2. To equip with skills to create</p>
<p>3. To express inner thoughts and emotions</p>
<p>4. To celebrate the vibrancy of life through meaningful connection with
society and culture</p>
<p></p>
<p>The department’s approach is to develop each student to be able to:</p>
<p>• perform the arts with the knowledge and skills learnt</p>
<p>• create arts, with knowledge and skills acquired</p>
<p>• respond to the arts</p>
<p>We aim to provide all students with quality arts education to enable them
to find an area of interest where they deepen their interest and build
confidence. We aim to create a vibrant arts environment to educate our
students to acquire knowledge, skills and values during their formative
years to enable them to develop and grow to their full potential.</p>
<p></p>
<p><strong>Talent Development Approach</strong>
</p>
<p>Our Talent Development Approach seeks to instill the joy of learning as
students discover their interests and strengths through the 3Es:</p>
<p></p>

<p><strong>Experience</strong>
</p>
<p>·&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; We create rich, varied experiences
that spark every student's love of learning. Through engaging classroom
lessons and immersive learning journeys, we ensure equal access to opportunities
that broaden perspectives and unlock each student's unique potential.</p>
<p><strong>Enrich</strong>
</p>
<p>·&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; We nurture students with emerging
interests and talents, fostering their passion whilst building confidence.
Through our enriched curriculum, dedicated afterschool enrichment programmes,
and fringe activities such as Recess Art, we create diverse pathways for
growth. Our comprehensive approach encompasses CCAs, school-based activities,
performances, and competitions that celebrate our students’ journey and
unlock their creative potential.</p>
<p><strong>Excel</strong>
</p>
<p>·&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; We provide enriching opportunities
that inspire students to discover and develop their unique strengths. Through
targeted training sessions and meaningful participation in inter-school
and national competitions or performances, we ensure access to platforms
that nurture special talents and unlock students’ potential for excellence.</p>
