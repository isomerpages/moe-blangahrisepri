<p>On 21 July, our school commemorated Racial Harmony Day with the theme, <strong>“Our People, Our Tapestry.”</strong> The
theme reminded us that Singapore’s multicultural society is like a vibrant
tapestry, woven together by the unique identities, traditions, beliefs
and practices of our diverse communities.</p>

<p>The celebration began with an engaging assembly programme featuring a
lively skit, songs performed in different languages and an interactive
quiz that tested students’ knowledge of Singapore’s rich cultural heritage.
Students also took part in a variety of hands-on activities, including
traditional games such as Five Stones and Chapteh, as well as a matching
game that introduced them to traditional Chinese, Malay and Indian foods.</p>
<p>Through these meaningful and enjoyable experiences, students gained a
deeper appreciation of Singapore’s rich cultural diversity and strengthened
their understanding of the importance of mutual respect, empathy and harmony.
As they celebrated the richness of different cultures, they were reminded
that by valuing one another’s differences and working together, we can
continue to keep Singapore’s multicultural tapestry vibrant, strong and
united.</p>
