<p>Our school celebrated EAS Day 2026 with the theme <em>“Celebrating Our Everyday Heroes”</em>,
honouring our Executive and Administrative Staff (EAS) for the dedication,
care and support they bring to our school community each day.</p>
<p>During the assembly, students learnt more about the different groups of
EAS staff and the important roles they play in keeping our school running
smoothly. The lively “Guess Who?” quiz added to the excitement, as students
used clues to identify familiar EAS staff members while discovering interesting
facts about them.</p>

<p>Students also expressed their appreciation for our EAS staff, thanking
them for the many ways they support and care for the school. The celebration
continued with fun-filled games, giving our EAS staff an opportunity to
relax, connect and enjoy the occasion together.</p>
